How your midterm grade is computed

Each activity is scored as a percentage of its items. Class Performance (60%) is the average of Quiz, Seatwork, Laboratory (Assignment for CC101) and Recitation, 25% each. Major Exam (40%) is your midterm exam percentage. The midterm grade is cut to two decimals, not rounded. Missing work counts as 0.
